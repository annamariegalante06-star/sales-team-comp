How You Get Paid

Simple. Fair. Rewards quality.

Two roles. One goal.

SDR — You find the leads

Your job is to find local businesses that need Relay. You reach out, qualify them, and either book a demo for Andy or get them into a trial.

You get paid when you find good leads. The better the lead, the more you earn.

AE (Andy) — You close the deals

Your job is to run demos and turn interested businesses into paying customers.

You get paid when you close deals. The longer customers stay, the more you earn.

Why we pay bonuses at month 3, 6, and 12

We analyzed every customer Relay has ever had. Here's what the data shows:

40%

leave before month 3. Average total revenue: $282

33%

make it to month 6. Average total revenue: $1,070

3.8x

more revenue from customers who hit month 6 vs. those who don't

Month 1-2: Danger zone (25% churn/month) → Month 3-5: Settling in (20% churn/month) → Month 6+: Locked in (under 8% churn/month)
That's why bonuses ratchet up: a small reward at month 3 (survived the danger zone), a big reward at month 6 (locked in), and another at month 12 (true partnership). Every bonus is earned, not given.

What counts as a lead?

Direct Sale Lead

You found a business, reached out, and they agreed to a demo.

  • ✓ Real local business with customers walking in
  • ✓ Talked to the owner or manager
  • ✓ They confirmed they can afford $99-185/month
  • ✓ Booked a demo time with Andy
  • ✗ "Let me think about it" without a booked time
  • ✗ Can't confirm budget

→ You earn: $5 when the demo is booked

Credit Card Trial Lead

Someone starts a 30-day free trial with their credit card.

  • ✓ Real business, not a test account
  • ✓ Card was accepted (not declined)
  • ✓ Can be someone you referred or organic
  • ✗ Duplicates don't count
  • ✗ Declined cards don't count

→ You earn: $5 when they sign up. $60 if they become a customer.

No Credit Card Trial Lead

Someone starts a free trial with just an email. No card.

  • ✓ Real business email
  • ✓ You earn a small bonus just for getting them in the door
  • ✗ Personal/test emails don't count
  • ✗ Duplicates don't count

→ You earn: $3 on signup. Up to $60 if they convert.

Organic / Self-Serve Lead

Someone finds Relay through email marketing, ads, or word of mouth and signs up on their own.

  • ✓ Real business that signed up through a marketing channel
  • ✓ Assigned to an SDR for follow-up and support
  • ✓ 90-day lookback: if an SDR previously sourced this lead, they get credit
  • ✗ Test/fake signups don't count

→ SDR earns: $5 on first contact. $40 on conversion.

Boomerang Lead

A churned customer comes back and reactivates their account.

  • ✓ Previously paying customer who cancelled
  • ✓ Reactivates on a paid plan
  • ✓ Milestone bonuses reset from zero (earn them again)
  • ✗ Must have been cancelled for 30+ days

→ SDR earns $30, AE earns $20 on reactivation. Residuals restart.

Would you walk into this business today and spend your own money? If yes, it's probably a lead.
